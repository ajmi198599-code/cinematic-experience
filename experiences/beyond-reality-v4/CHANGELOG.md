# V4 UX redesign — One Click, One Shop, Product in Front

**Reason:** Previous versions overemphasised portals, coloured lights and pseudo-3D effects. Customer goal: enter easily, click a section, and see products in front of you immediately.

**Implemented:** landing immediately in the mall; 3 clear store entrances; full-size product display on one tap; product carousel and swipe; shop switching; bilingual interface; all-product search; demo basket; static JSON product data; responsive styling in a neutral/warm retail palette.

**Removed from main shopper route:** mandatory whale, fantasy-world selection, finale, fake corridor walking. These remain in prior independent sites and branches.

**Not done:** free-roaming 3D mall, real-world product images and stock, secure payment, verified commercial licensing, real mobile performance certification.
