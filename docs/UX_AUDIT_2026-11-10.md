# BEYOND REALITY — UX & Commercial Readiness Audit

**Date:** 10 November 2026  
**Subject:** [Beyond Reality Gallery](https://beyond-reality-gallery.vercel.app/#mall)  
**Scope:** Cinematic journey (Chapters 01–04), photographic shopping gallery (Chapter 05), bilingual search, catalogue filtering, details, basket and content replacement.  
**Method:** Source inspection, automated Chromium interaction tests at widths **360, 390, 430, 768 and 1440 pixels**, keyboard checks and offline-photo-fallback simulation. These tests **are not user research, a real-device performance benchmark, a legal review, or full WCAG certification.**

## Executive summary

The project is **strong as an interactive creative showcase**. The portal, underwater world, three universes and finale provide a memorable narrative; the gallery has the foundations of a usable product browser. The purchase journey is *not* production-ready: nine products, prices and photos are illustrative; there is no stock, checkout, merchant integration or order fulfilment.

Our main UX direction is **dual-route navigation**: keep the cinematic journey for first-time visitors, but offer shoppers a clear **Skip to Showroom** route. A compelling sales experience requires people to **find, understand, trust, add, modify and (eventually) buy** products quickly.

## UX journey assessment

| Step | What visitors can do | Finding / opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Arrival | Enter a glowing portal | Delightful for exploration, but slow if someone is trying to buy something; add a showroom shortcut |
| 2. Ocean | Explore underwater scene and touch ripples | Keep animations optional and respect reduced-motion settings; avoid blocking the way forward |
| 3. Choose world | Explore Fire / Nature / Space | Current navigation is primarily a guided narrative; a visible progress indicator or hub navigation would help orientation |
| 4. Finale | View closing scene, continue or replay | Add a clear next action to visit the showroom |
| 5. Showroom | Browse three categories, search, sort, inspect products | Excellent starting point; improve touch targets, feedback, empty results, accurate images and copy |
| 6. Basket | Add or change quantity, review indicative total | Make edits understandable and accessible; session persistence is useful, but a real checkout is not yet available |

**Important distinction:** The current mall is a **clickable 2D showroom**, not a free-roaming 3D shopping centre. To build genuine walking-style navigation later, we should add a lightweight corridor with entry hotspots, a mini-map, back-to-corridor control, shop signs, keyboard/touch equivalents and a normal product-list fallback. Full 3D should be reserved for clear customer benefit.

## Content, text and replacement plan

Before a sale-ready launch, each product must have verifiable content, not placeholder branding or AI-generated specifications. Use the accompanying **CATALOGUE_REPLACEMENT_TEMPLATE.csv** as a *worksheet*, not an automatically connected store database. A production catalogue should contain:

1. **Product identification:** permanent SKU, supplier name, brand permission, category, name in Arabic and English, model/variant/size.
2. **Verified product data:** description, exact specifications, dimensions, materials, warranty, reliable stock and availability.
3. **Genuine media:** original high-resolution image files, usage permission, licence/credit evidence, alt text in Arabic and English, image-to-SKU match.
4. **Accurate pricing:** SAR price, whether VAT is included, discount validity, shipping cost, offer expiry and returns conditions.
5. **Operations:** inventory, secure orders and payments, deliveries, customer support, privacy, terms and required Saudi-market compliance review.

**Add a product:** create the verified record and images, preview the card and detail view, validate Arabic/English copy, test search and basket, then publish. **Replace a product:** keep the SKU stable only when it is genuinely the same item; otherwise use a new SKU and remove the old listing. **Remove a product:** archive it safely, maintain order-history references in a future backend, and do not reuse identifiers improperly. The current demo has no order histories or real inventory.

|---|---|

## Usability walk-through scripts for real participants

Ask participants to complete tasks **without hints**. Observe where they hesitate and whether they understand the showroom is currently a prototype.

1. **Find without the film:** Open the home page and reach the shopping catalogue in under two interactions. Measure time and errors.
2. **Search:** Find tea using English and Arabic, including “شاي” and “شاى”. Try a misspelling and note whether results help recovery.
3. **Filter:** Find a product in Style & Motion, then remove filters. Confirm the number of visible products makes sense.
4. **Inspect:** Open a detail view, identify the exact product and understand the price and availability. At present, they should recognise it is illustrative.
5. **Basket:** Add an item, increase and decrease quantity, clear the basket and return to browsing. Confirm feedback and focus are obvious.
6. **Mobile accessibility:** Use larger text, VoiceOver or TalkBack, keyboard navigation, RTL mode and reduced motion. Record any trap or unreadable label.
7. **Walkthrough:** Navigate portal → ocean → one chosen universe → finale → showroom and find the way back.

## Release criteria

**Prototype publication (now):** successful navigation, no blocking script errors, functional search/basket, accessible dialog focus, 44+ px core touch controls, appropriate demo disclaimers and public Vercel link.

**Content launch (later):** all listed photos/licences/SKUs/specifications verified, storefront labels factual, working inventory and documented returns/shipping policies. No fake scarcity, ratings or discounts.

**Commercial launch (later):** tested real checkout/payment, fulfilment and refunds, error/recovery states, privacy/security and local legal review, device/browser/accessibility tests and an operational support process.

## Measurement plan and limitations

Once actual customer testing is allowed, instrument (with suitable notice/consent) **arrival-to-gallery skip rate, gallery entry, category selection, search use, zero-result rate, detail opens, add-to-cart rate, basket edits, checkout starts and completed purchases**. Measure Web Vitals (**LCP, INP, CLS**) on real mobile connections. Do not invent scores before gathering data.

### Final verdict

**Creative showcase:** complete and worth keeping. **Shopping prototype:** usable, with fixes implemented in a separate UX preview. **Real ecommerce:** not ready until verified products, content rights, order/payment/stock integrations and operational/legal checks exist.
