02Use compressed images, videos, and 3D models where appropriate. Avoid massive GIF files for large backgrounds.

Earlier concept targets: about 3–8 MB for an initial mall visit and 5–20 MB per store as it loads. These are planning examples, not promises; our final targets depend on actual assets and testing.

A VERY SIMPLE PATH FORWARD

From idea to live website.

We don't have to build the entire universe or shopping centre on day one.

PHASE 01

One wow moment

Build the opening portal and one beautiful transformation. Test touch and scroll on a phone.

PHASE 02

One interactive journey

Add the three world choices, or one mall corridor and one functional sample shop.

PHASE 03

Publish and expand

Organise the code in GitHub, deploy on Vercel, test the load speed, then add more scenes gradually.

THE IDEA IN ONE SENTENCE

Make people feel inside the website.

Not by making everything complicated—by making each transition, choice and movement feel intentional.

An interactive explanation of two creative concepts. No real shops, checkout, products, sound, heavy 3D, tracking, or external downloads. Works offline.
